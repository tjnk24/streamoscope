> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Coming from 0.10.0 or older, and an update said the new version didn't start and went back? Run the installer once.** As in 0.10.1 and later: on some PCs OBS opens the dock later than old versions were willing to wait. Close OBS and run [streamoscope-setup-0.11.1.exe](https://github.com/tjnk24/streamoscope/releases/download/v0.11.1/streamoscope-setup-0.11.1.exe) over your installation: it installs without that check. Your settings, the journal and OBS stay as they are. If your updates go through fine, skip it.

- **The "StreamoScope updated" toast now has a close button.** A cross on the right puts it away at once, for those who'd rather not watch it for eight seconds.
